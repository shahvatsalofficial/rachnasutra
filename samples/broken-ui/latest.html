RS-0013 — Animations or transitions exist but no prefers-reduced-motion handling

Severity / confidence / status
medium / high / confirmed
Criterion
WCAG 2.2: 2.3.3 Animation from Interactions (AAA, best practice)
Evidence
styles.css:6 
.fade { transition: opacity 0.4s; animation: spin 2s infinite; }
User impact
Users with vestibular disorders may be made unwell by motion they asked the OS to reduce.
Recommended fix
Wrap non-essential motion in @media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference) or disable it under reduce.
Verification
Enable "reduce motion" in the OS and confirm motion stops.
Limitations
Motion may be handled in JavaScript; only CSS was scanned.
